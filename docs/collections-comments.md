# Collections, Comments and Scheduled Lives

## Collections

A collection is a playlist: a series, an album, a course. In the Library, open **Collections**, give it a title, pick files and put them in order. Each collection gets its own page at `/c/<wallet>/collection/<id>` with a player that moves to the next file by itself, and it appears on your creator page.

- Up to 30 collections, up to 100 files each.
- Saving needs one free wallet signature. Only files that really exist in your Library can be added.
- Deleting a collection never deletes the files.

## Comments

Every media page has comments. Anyone can read them. Posting needs a connected wallet and one free signature, so each comment is tied to a wallet and cannot be forged.

- Up to 500 characters. A wallet can post about six comments a minute.
- The author and the owner of the page can delete a comment.
- Comments show the author's profile name when they have one.
- Paid files have comments too, so fans can talk about them.

## Scheduled lives

On **Go Live** you can schedule a stream: a title and a time, up to 30 days ahead and up to five at a time. It shows on your creator page and in the **Upcoming lives** row on Discover, with a countdown in the viewer's own time. Followers with live alerts on are told when you schedule it.

- A scheduled live stays listed for two hours after its start time, then disappears.
- Scheduling does not start the stream. When the time comes you press **Go live** as usual, and followers are alerted then too.
- There is no reminder at the start time, because the free hosting plan cannot run timed jobs. This is on the roadmap.

## Watch time

Video and audio pages count how long people really play them, in small pieces. Your Library shows watch time per file and your Home page shows the total. It is an estimate, because anyone can send a count. Each report is capped, rate limited and tied to a file, and nothing identifies the viewer.
